01 · Who we are

We turn Ghana's most abundant resource into measurable savings.

Accra · West Africa

Four Points Green Power is a Ghanaian solar energy and green power company headquartered in Accra. We design, supply, install, and maintain solar systems for residential, commercial, corporate, and off-grid community clients across Ghana.

The name speaks to our compass-bearing identity: serving every direction, every segment, and every scale of need with the same standard of care. The green power is our purpose — transforming how Ghanaians power their lives by harnessing the sun.

07 · Our team

The people behind the power.

A multidisciplinary team combining decades of engineering, finance, strategy, and environmental expertise — united by a single mission: powering Ghana's green energy transition.

CKA
Clifford Kofi Amissah
Managing Director

Clifford is the founder and Managing Director of Four Points Green Power Ltd, committed to making clean, affordable energy accessible to homes and businesses across Ghana. With a background spanning environmental consulting, business development, and renewable energy, he leads the company's overall strategy, client relationships, and operations. Passionate about Ghana's green energy transition, Clifford founded Four Points Green Power to offer world-class solar solutions that deliver real savings and lasting impact.

SAM
Sylvester A-Mawuena
Finance & Business Development

Sylvester brings over three decades of financial and business management expertise to Four Points Green Power. A Member of the Chartered Management Institute (UK) and holder of an MBA from the University of South Wales, he has built his career across telecoms, microfinance, and business consultancy — including 22 years as a Financial Analyst at Vodafone Ghana. He oversees the company's financial strategy, business planning, and growth initiatives.

AE
Andre Egar
Business Strategy & Environmental Affairs

Andre is a finance, environmental, and business strategy consultant with over a decade of experience advising SMEs, development organisations, and investment firms across Ghana. Holding an MSc in Environmental Sustainability and Management, he has worked with USAID, DFID, and the Millennium Development Authority. At Four Points Green Power, he drives strategic direction and ensures our projects align with the highest environmental and sustainability standards.

AKE
Astrea Kwame Essandoh Jnr.
Project Engineer

Astrea is a seasoned Electrical Engineer with over three decades of experience across industrial, commercial, and renewable energy sectors. Having worked across Ghana, the Seychelles, and the United States, he brings a global perspective and deep technical expertise to every project. Trained in Solar PV, Solar Water Heating, Micro-Hydro, and Electric Vehicles in the USA, he leads technical project execution — from system design and installation to commissioning and maintenance.
